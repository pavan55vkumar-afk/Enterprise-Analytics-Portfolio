CASE STUDY 03 · PRODUCT ANALYTICS

Checkout Conversion A/B Test

PythonStatisticsExperimentation
THE PROBLEM

A conversion increase could have been random noise.

The product team needed evidence before exposing all users to a new checkout experience.

WHAT I DID

Evaluated lift and statistical significance.

Compared 5,000 users, calculated conversion rates and lift, and performed a two-proportion z-test.

THE SOLUTION

Recommended a controlled rollout with guardrails.

Combined statistical evidence with device performance, revenue-per-visitor and experience metrics.

CONTROL 11.82% 293 conversions / 2,478 users
RELATIVE LIFT +24.7% +2.93 percentage points
VARIANT 14.75% 372 conversions / 2,522 users RECOMMENDED
Z-statistic3.046
P-value0.00232
Significance level0.05
DecisionStatistically significant
RECOMMENDATION

Proceed with a staged rollout while tracking conversion by device, revenue per visitor, errors, refunds and page performance.

ANALYTICAL OUTCOME

Provided statistically supported evidence for the variant while keeping operational and customer-experience risks visible.
